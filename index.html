Same place or not
—

Which parts of a street do you use to recognise it?

Twenty pairs of street-level photographs from Copenhagen, San Francisco and Tokyo. For each pair you decide whether the two pictures show the same place, and then point at whatever made you decide.

  1. Judge. Same place, or different places.
  2. Rate how sure you are, from 1 to 5.
  3. Mark your evidence. Click directly on each photograph, once to three times, wherever you see what settled it — whether that is something shared or something that does not match. Then say what kind of thing you marked.

Every pair has a correct answer, drawn from the camera positions recorded when the photographs were taken. The point of the study is not your score; it is where you looked. Roughly ten minutes.
